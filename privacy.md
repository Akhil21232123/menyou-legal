Menyou is an independent student-built dining app for the Colby College community. It is not owned or operated by Colby College or Colby Dining Services. Questions: menyou.verify@gmail.com.

## What we collect

Account info (name, email), dietary preferences, allergies, taste ratings, dining-hall check-ins, optional profile photo, and technical logs needed to run the app. Menu data is collected from publicly available Colby dining sources (including Nutrislice).

## Campus dining counts

If you agree during signup, Menyou counts your de-identified dining activity (which hall and meal, which dishes you saw or rated, and whether you checked in) in aggregate statistics we may share with the campus dining operator. Those statistics do not include your name, email, photo, or chat. You can say no and still use Menyou. You can change this later in Profile. Account deletion removes your raw events.

We do not sell your personal data. The counts above are totals. They are shared only if you opted in. Leaving the box unchecked, or turning it off later in Profile → Account Details, keeps you out of those totals.

## Friends

If you add friends, we store that list and any pending requests. Friends see your first name, last name, and optional photo, not your email, allergies, or taste scores. Eat together averages your existing Match% values and shows only a combined hall ranking; nobody sees anyone else's personal score. Same-hall chat still requires both of you to check in at that hall. We do not share live location, GPS, or a map of where you are.

## Location

Menyou does not collect GPS or live location. The dining hall you tap after a meal is the record. We never pick a hall from your phone, never show a map of where you are, and never use location to forecast how many students will check in.

## How we use it

To personalize menu recommendations, calculate match scores, send on-device meal reminders you opt into, keep the service working, and improve Menyou. We do not sell your personal data.

## Storage and security

Data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth. API requests are rate-limited. Campus meal timing in the app always uses America/New_York, not your phone's time zone.

## How long we keep it

Raw product analytics are kept for 180 days, then deleted. Taste ratings stay until you delete the account, because they train Match%. Aggregate dining counts have no name attached and may be kept for 24 months. Push tokens, chat messages, and email addresses are not included in the aggregate file.

## Third parties

We use Nutrislice/Colby Dining for menus and Unsplash or similar sources for food images. Meal reminders use on-device notifications. Image lookups may use curated URLs and optional image APIs. Those services have their own terms. If you opt in, de-identified dining counts may be shared with the campus dining operator as described above.

## Your choices

Update preferences anytime in Profile. The campus-counts choice is under Profile → Account Details. Delete your account under Account Details → Delete Account to permanently remove your data, subject to backups and legal holds. You can ignore friend requests and remove friends anytime.
