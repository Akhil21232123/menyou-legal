# Menyou support

Effective and last updated: October 24, 2026

Menyou is a student-built dining app for Colby. We cannot see your password. Do not send it.

**Email:** menyou.verify@gmail.com

Include the Colby email on the account and what you were doing (signing up, a missing menu, a crash). We reply from that address.

**Delete your account:** open Menyou → Profile → Account Details → Delete Account. Deletion removes your profile and preference data from our systems, including removal from backups within 30 days, as described in the Privacy Policy. Aggregate counts that were already totaled do not contain your name.

**Campus dining counts:** When you create an account, Menyou records anonymized, aggregated dining activity: which hall and meal, which dishes you saw or rated, and whether you checked in. These totals do not include your name, email, photo, or chat messages. We may share the totals with the campus dining operator. Account deletion removes your raw events; totals that are already combined have no name attached. Anonymized dining totals are part of the Terms and Privacy Policy you agree to at signup, and there is no in-app opt-out. If you do not agree, do not create an account. Existing accounts that have not recorded this agreement are asked once on the next launch.

Read the [Terms of Service](../terms/) and [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).

**Allergies:** Menyou does not decide what is safe to eat. Check the dining hall before you eat.

**Menus and hours:** halls publish their own menus. If a dish is missing, it may not have been posted yet.
