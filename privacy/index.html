Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 25, 2026

What we collect

Account details: your name, email, Colby ID, a password (stored only as a salted hash), and an optional profile photo. Your phone's time zone.

Food preferences: dietary preferences, allergies, and your taste quiz answers and ratings.

What you do in the app: dishes you rate, This or That picks, dining-hall check-ins and meal history, streaks, the text you type into search, friends and friend requests, Eat Together party membership, same-hall chat messages, and which screens, buttons, and dishes you open or see.

Notifications: your device's push notification token, if you allow notifications.

Technical data: your IP address, which we use for rate limiting and abuse prevention, plus error reports and server logs needed to run the app.

Menu data is collected from publicly available Colby dining sources, including Nutrislice.

Guest users

If you use Menyou without an account, we do not ask for a name or email and your browsing is not linked to one. Your device still contacts our servers, so we see technical data such as your IP address, which we use to run and secure the app.

Campus dining counts

When you create an account, Menyou records your dining activity and ties it to your account: which hall and meal, which dishes you saw, rated, or picked, what you search for, and whether you checked in. We use it to run and improve Menyou and its services. Before anything is shared outside Menyou, we combine it into anonymous totals that do not include your name, email, photo, or chat messages, and we may share those totals with the campus dining operator. We may also use aggregated data to improve Menyou and its services. The raw activity stays linked to your account until you delete the account. Account deletion removes it; totals that are already combined have no name attached and may be kept.

Friends

If you add friends, we store that list and any pending requests. Friends, and people who search for you by name, see your first name, last name, and optional photo, not your email, allergies, or taste scores. Anyone using Menyou can find other people by name.

The campus leaderboard shows other signed-in users your first name, how many meals you have rated, and your streak. Friends can see which hall you checked in at when they check in for the same meal, and we may tell a friend when you share the same top Match% hall.

Eat Together averages existing Match% values into a combined hall ranking. In a group of only two people, the result can reveal the other person's score. Same-hall chat requires both of you to check in at that hall. We do not share live location, GPS, or a map of where you are.

Notifications

With your permission, Menyou sends push notifications through Apple's Push Notification service: meal suggestions around lunch (or weekend brunch) and dinner, and alerts for friend requests, a friend at the same hall, party invites, chat messages, and streaks. We store your device token to send them. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.

Location

The current version of Menyou does not collect GPS or live location. The dining hall you tap after a meal is the record. The app does not pick a hall from your phone, show a map of where you are, or use location to forecast how many students will check in. Records from earlier versions of the app may still exist and are removed when you delete your account.

Do Not Track

Menyou does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not allow third parties to do so. Because we do not engage in this kind of tracking, we do not change our practices in response to browser Do Not Track signals.

How we use it

To personalize menu recommendations, calculate match scores, send notifications you allow, run friends, Eat Together, chat, and the leaderboard, keep the service working and secure (including rate limiting and abuse prevention), review reports, and improve Menyou and its services. We may use aggregated data to improve Menyou and its services. We do not sell your personal data.

Storage and security

Data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security. Our server runs on Render behind Cloudflare. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash, and API requests are rate-limited by IP address. Campus meal timing in the app always uses America/New_York time, not your phone's time zone. Menyou's operators can see account-linked activity when running and supporting the service.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, but no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a breach that affects your personal data, we will notify affected users by email or in the app without undue delay, and within any time limit required by law.

Third parties

We use Nutrislice / Colby Dining for menus. Food images come from the Spoonacular API, Pexels, and Unsplash or similar sources. The app loads these images directly from those services' servers, so they can see your device's IP address. Images are illustrative and may not show the actual dish served. Notifications are sent through Apple's Push Notification service.

Service providers that process data on our behalf: Supabase (database, login, and file storage), Render (server hosting), Cloudflare (network delivery and protection), and Brevo (sends sign-up and verification emails, so it sees your email address). These services have their own terms, and those that process personal data for us must protect it at least as well as this policy does.

Data retention

Unless noted here, we keep your data until you delete your account.

Same-hall chat messages are deleted automatically after 24 hours. In-app notifications are deleted after a couple of days. Some detailed usage events are deleted automatically after a set period (currently 90 to 180 days depending on the type). Sign-up codes and rate-limit records are temporary, but may stay for a while before they are cleaned up. Reports about an account may be kept after deletion to protect other users.

When you delete your account, we remove your profile, preferences, ratings, activity, friend links, and messages from our active systems, and your profile photo from storage, unless we must keep something to meet a legal obligation. Copies in backups are removed on a rolling basis as backups expire. Aggregate totals that are already combined have no name attached and may be kept. If we permanently discontinue Menyou, we will delete all personal data within 6 months.

Children

Menyou is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it. If you believe this has happened, contact us at menyou.verify@gmail.com.

Your choices and rights

Update your preferences anytime in Profile, and turn off notifications in your phone's settings. You can ignore friend requests and remove or block friends anytime. The activity data and totals described above are part of the Terms and Privacy Policy you agree to at signup, and there is no in-app opt-out.

To get a copy of your data or ask us to correct it, email menyou.verify@gmail.com from your account email. We will respond within 30 days. To withdraw your consent and permanently remove your data, delete your account under Profile → Account Details → Delete Account.

Business transfers

If Menyou is incorporated, merged, acquired, or sold, your data may transfer to the successor, which will continue to protect it under this Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, the "Last updated" date will change, and material changes will be announced in the app.
